# The sprite engine and its conventions

> **Read when:** you are going to look at, render, analyse or later author Clawd animations. **Related:** [identity](identity.md) · [timing & spacing](../foundations/timing-and-spacing.md)

Source: `plugins/clawd-buddy-gauges/hooks/crew-sprites.js` (about 2,900 lines) plus `crew-pet.ts` (which animation shows when) and `pet-client.tsx`. This page only describes; nothing here asks for a change.

## The medium in one table

| Thing | Value |
|---|---|
| Grid | **24 x 20** palette-indexed cells per frame; only rows 0-17 are drawn into the SVG (the last two rows only held a tile shadow) |
| Pixel shape | square units in the SVG; the engine's comments say the CLI logo's own quadrants are twice as tall as wide, which is where the 12x8 body proportion came from |
| Body | top-left at (6, 8), 12 x 8; ground row 18; legs reach the ground two rows below the body |
| Parts | body, eyes, two "arms" (claws), four legs, **one prop** (more only for crowds), optionally a hat |
| Palette | indices 1-13 plus four added for hats: body, shade, ink, white, greys, blue, green, red, yellow, light grey, brown, small-Clawd tone |
| Stage | tile `#1b1a19`, floor line `#2c2a27` |
| Output | each animation is a list of frames and one **ms per frame** (110-500 ms in the catalogue) |
| Catalogue | 17 moods, 71 looks (3-7 per mood), plus 5 hats worn over any look as `name+hat` |
| Rendering | the band builds a self-contained SVG: runs of same-coloured cells become one path per colour, frames flip with SMIL `visibility` animation (discrete), `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`. A terminal renderer draws from the same grids |

## The authoring vocabulary

Each frame is a small object: `{ dx, dy, breathe, squash, lie, legs, armL, armR, eyes, color, prop(s) }`.

- **eyes:** open (default), wide, closed, wink, happy, x, squished, dot, and the directions up/down/left/right and diagonals (see [faces & eyes](../foundations/faces-and-eyes.md)).
- **arms:** rest, mid, up, down, none.
- **legs:** stand, tuck, walkA, walkB, none.
- **squash:** positive = wider and shorter (area about 96), negative = a stretch; **lie** flattens onto the ground with legs tucked.
- **dx/dy:** shift the whole body (shuffle, hop); **breathe:** body sinks one row onto shortened legs.
- **prop:** drawn *behind* Clawd unless it is in the HELD list (mug, blanket, cube, letter, shades, keyboard, newspaper, tablet, binoculars, crate, walkers, star, line, members, map, hammer, sign, trophy, vacuum), which are drawn *in front*. Each prop takes a **step** that advances per frame (dots filling, a speech bubble growing lines).
- **hats:** rows of characters whose last row sits on his head, drawn in every frame except for looks that already wear something.

Because everything is composed from the same parts, **a new look is a recipe, not a redraw**, which is why the roster stays on-model. It's also why the catalogue favours changes the parts can express and why truly new shapes need new prop or part code.

## Time

One fixed ms per animation; holds are repeated frames (see [timing](../foundations/timing-and-spacing.md#look-at-it-how-our-engine-handles-time)). Moods wait `HOLD_MS` = 2 s before changing unless the new or the current mood is urgent (waiting, done, error). A random look from the mood is picked; morning leans to coffee and stretch, night to yawn; a click steps through the mood's looks in order.

## Things to know before analysing

- **Frames are grids.** To study motion, compare grids (the figures in [examples/](../examples/) do this) rather than reading code.
- **The generated SVGs in docs/pets/** are the same art at 28x22 on a tile with rounded corners; the band's own render has no tile.
- **Rendering tool for this KB:** the figures were generated by a small script (kept out of the repo) that imports the engine, picks frames, collapses repeats and writes SVG. To regenerate after sprite changes, write a similar script; there is no build step to maintain.

## Constraints that shape the art

- A 24-wide canvas and a 12-wide body leave about 6 columns each side for props, claws and effects. Very wide poses (the flattened "lie" at depth 7, for example) hit the canvas edge.
- Colour is limited to the palette array; accents are for props and effects.
- Anything that has to read in the band must read at roughly 1x (the SVG is scaled to the cell it is given).
