# References, trust grades and the UNVERIFIED ledger

> How far to trust each source, what was verified, and what wasn't. If you add a claim to the KB, add its source here.

**Grades.** **A** = the creator or an official/primary source, or a peer-reviewed abstract opened directly. **B** = reputable secondary (journalism, Wikipedia, detailed breakdowns) or a practitioner's blog. **C** = unsigned or vendor/SEO guide sites and fan pages: useful and often right, but numbers are rules of thumb and the sites repeat each other. **S** = seen only as a search snippet, never opened.

Research date: 2026-11-25. Raw notes with extra detail per area: [research/](research/).

Starting point for the craft layer: [pixeli.pebbly.space/knowledge](https://pixeli.pebbly.space/knowledge), a rulebook for AI agents drawing in Aseprite (62 rules, 14 skills, 4 agents).
