# Tensions: where good advice disagrees with itself

> **Read when:** two principles pull opposite ways, or a source's rule doesn't fit what you want to make. A tension isn't a problem to resolve once; it's a dial to set on purpose. **Related:** [review protocol](review-protocol.md)

| Tension | One side protects | The other side protects | How the research broke it on purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Readable vs personal** | state reads at a glance | charm, ambiguity, projection | Pac-Man and Hello Kitty leave the face blank; Clawd keeps a blank face but gives the body and props the legibility |
| **Constant body vs distinct silhouettes** | identity | state legibility | Kirby holds eye spacing while the body squashes; Clawd varies prop and claws |
| **Few colours vs expression** | cohesion, identity | range of states | Shovel Knight's added "cheater" colours; an accent used only for state |
| **No dither / no AA at 16 px** | clean clusters | texture, smoothness | Saint11 dithered a flannel shirt; one deliberate AA pixel can save a curve |
| **Outline for readability vs room to draw** | separation from background | pixels for the form | solid shapes with strong contrast instead of outlines at extreme low-res (Saint11); Clawd has none |
| **Symmetry is calm vs asymmetry is alive** | stability, reassurance | life, thought | a single deliberate asymmetry (a wink, one raised claw); symmetric rest, asymmetric event |
| **Squash accuracy vs exaggeration** | believability | comedy, speed | the press in "compacting" flattens far past volume conservation, on purpose |
| **Anticipation vs latency** | weight, readability of action | responsiveness | a 1-frame cue, or the squash frame that already leaves the ground (Saint11, UNVERIFIED) |
| **Alive vs ignorable** | charm | peripheral calm | cooldowns, probabilities, rest as a behaviour (Mochi, Neko) |
| **Needy vs charming** | a real call to act | not nagging | escalate once, then settle (Tamagotchi's record of an ignored call) |
| **Consistent grammar vs quirky shapes** | readable roster | surprise | keep the grammar fixed, vary silhouette detail (Animal Crossing) |
| **Cute vs competent** | warmth | trust | boxy upright stance and work props for competence; gags in calm states for warmth |
| **Joke vs interruption** | delight | focus | jokes in calm and failure states, brief |
| **Formula vs freshness** | a tested structure | surprise | the rule of three is a tool; vary it |
| **Frame count vs energy** | smoothness | punch | delete the weakest frame, lengthen the rest (Slynyrd) |
| **Fixed ms per animation vs expressive holds** | simplicity | timing precision | repeated frames at a small quantum; know your quantum |
| **Realism vs readability** (pets) | body language | icon legibility at 32x16 | mix a tiny pose with a tiny icon |
| **Rules vs the work** | consistency | the idea | all of the above |

## How to use a tension

1. **Name the two sides in this specific sprite.** ("Readable vs personal: I want the bubble to say 'needs you' fast, but I also want a smirk.")
2. **Decide which side the *moment* needs.** An urgent state leans legible; a calm state can lean personal.
3. **Let one side lead, and keep a bit of the other.** A legible urgent state can still have a wink.
4. **Write the decision down** so the next person can overrule it knowingly.

## When a rule just doesn't fit

If you're about to follow a number that your eyes say is wrong, trust your eyes, after checking at 1x. The numbers in this KB mostly come from unsigned guide sites that repeat each other ([references](../references.md)); they're good starting points, not limits.
