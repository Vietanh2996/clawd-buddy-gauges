# Celeste: Madeline's hair as a gauge

> **Read when:** you want a sprite to show state without a HUD. **Template:** constraint → choices → sacrificed → what reads → what to steal (not copy).


## Constraint
A small pixel-art character on a modern engine whose state (how many dashes remain) the player must read mid-action.

## Choices
Hair colour is the gauge: red for one dash, pink for two, white during a dash, cyan when none remain (Celeste wiki). A dash lasts 15 frames with 3 freeze frames, a hit-pause that gives weight. She also sweats when stamina is low and her hair blows in the wind.

## What reads
The hair is the largest, most mobile, most saturated mass on a small character and sits in the player's focal area. A colour change is readable at 8-16 px; a drawn change wouldn't be.

## What to steal
- **Put state in the biggest saturated mass.** Try not to make the viewer look somewhere else.
- Pair colour with motion or shape so it isn't colour alone.
- Hit-pause (a frozen frame or two) gives impact for free.

## Caution
Fan commentary claims the hair is generated procedurally as a chain of circles over a bald base sprite; that is **UNVERIFIED** (not from the developers). Pixeli's "12 colours for Madeline" figure is UNVERIFIED too (one search found a 9-colour fan extraction). Saint11's own talk on Celeste's art could not be retrieved.

## For Clawd
His whole body is the saturated mass. The error moods already use **body tint as state** (darker shade); a claw tint or a shell tint for another state would be the same idea. See [value, colour & outline](../foundations/value-color-and-outline.md).
